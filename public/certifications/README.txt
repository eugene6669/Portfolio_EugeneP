Certifications Folder - Image Guidelines
=========================================

Add your certification images to this folder:

Required Images:
- bsit.jpg - Bachelor of Science in Information Technology certificate
- azure.jpg - Microsoft Azure Fundamentals certificate (if you have it)
- nodejs.jpg - Node.js Application Development certificate (if you have it)

Image Specifications:
- Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (1920x1080 recommended)
- File size: Keep under 1MB for optimal loading
- Quality: High resolution scans or screenshots of certificates

Tips:
- Scan or photograph your certificates in good lighting
- Ensure text is readable in the image
- You can use placeholder images initially and replace them later
- The portfolio will show a default award icon if images are not yet added

Images will appear on the Certifications page at /#certifications
