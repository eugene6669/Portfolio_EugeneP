About Section - Profile Image Guidelines
=========================================

Add your profile photo to this folder:

Required Image:
- profile.jpg - Your professional profile photograph

Image Specifications:
- Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 (square) - 800x800 or 1000x1000 recommended
- File size: Keep under 500KB for optimal loading
- Quality: High resolution, professional headshot

Tips:
- Use a professional-looking photo
- Good lighting and clean background preferred
- Crop to square aspect ratio for best results
- The image should show your face clearly
- Business casual or professional attire recommended

The profile image will appear on the About page at /#about
If no image is provided, a placeholder icon will be displayed.
