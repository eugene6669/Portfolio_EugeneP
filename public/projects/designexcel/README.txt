DesignExcel Furniture E-Commerce System - Image Guidelines
===========================================================

Add 3 images to this folder:
- 1.jpg - Homepage or main product catalog view
- 2.jpg - Product detail page or shopping cart
- 3.jpg - Admin dashboard or order management

Recommended image specifications:
- Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (1920x1080 recommended)
- File size: Keep under 500KB for optimal loading
- Quality: High resolution screenshots or mockups

Images will appear in a carousel on your portfolio website.
